# PRODUCT.md — Anshu Sarkar Portfolio

## Product
Personal portfolio of Anshu Sarkar, software engineer (frontend-leaning full stack, GenAI-curious). Static site on Next.js 15 + Tailwind v4 + GSAP, deployed on Vercel.

## Audience & scene
Recruiters, hiring managers, and fellow engineers reviewing Anshu for frontend / full-stack / AI-product roles. They arrive from a résumé link or LinkedIn, skim for 30–90 seconds, often on a phone; some open on a laptop to actually play with the demos.

## Job of the site
Prove craft fast: show shipped work (Skill.Test, TechPulse), evidence of real engineering experience (cvDragon, PepHub, internships), and make contact/resume effortless. Success = a visitor can describe at least one project an hour later and can grab the resume in one click.

## Proof / real content
- Projects: Skill.Test (AI interview platform, Mux demo video), TechPulse (FastAPI + Elasticsearch news search)
- Experience: PepHub Consultancy (SDE), cvDragon, Pearl Thoughts, WEBAXD
- Assets: project screenshots in /public/assets/projectImages, resume images /Resume.jpg + /ResumeLow.jpg, Google Drive resume link
- Contact: email, LinkedIn, GitHub, X — all real, linked in footer

## Constraints
- Keep all factual copy, links, and assets truthful; no invented claims
- Experience surface: the work leads; interface recedes
- Motion: simple, pervasive, respectful of prefers-reduced-motion
- Stack fixed: Next.js App Router, Tailwind v4, GSAP (ScrollSmoother licensed-free era)

## Design direction (user-pinned)
Risograph Print Studio — full replacement world chosen by the user on 2026-08-26. Paper ground, fluorescent-pink + riso-blue two-ink identity, grain, registration marks, misregistration as the signature interaction. See DESIGN.md once written.
